# Global Company Intelligence Agent

AI-driven global company intelligence platform for discovering, understanding, scoring, clustering, and explaining public discussion about companies across languages, regions, and source types.

## Vision

Turn fragmented public discussion into evidence-backed company intelligence. A user provides a company, brand, ticker, product, or related entity; the system discovers relevant public conversations across authorized data sources, resolves the company entity, analyzes multilingual content, deduplicates narratives, scores sentiment/impact/risk, detects trends, and returns every material insight with its original source URL.

## Core principles

- Evidence first: every material claim must trace to source records.
- Original-source preservation: source URLs are captured from ingestion and never invented by AI.
- Global by design: multilingual, regional, and local-source discovery are first-class capabilities.
- Sentiment is not impact: emotional polarity and importance/reach are separate dimensions.
- Narrative-aware: syndicated articles, reposts, and copies should not be counted as independent opinions.
- Explainable: scores and insights must expose supporting evidence and confidence.
- Compliance-aware: connectors respect platform APIs, licensing, robots rules, rate limits, privacy requirements, and access controls.

## Target output

The platform should provide company-level intelligence such as overall sentiment, positive/neutral/negative distribution, discussion volume and velocity, emerging topics, aspect sentiment, intent and emotion, reputation/product/customer/employee/regulatory risk, geography, source credibility, impact, trends, narrative clusters, and evidence-backed explanations.
